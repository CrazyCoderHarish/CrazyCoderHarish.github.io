My first TEDx talk, “From Noticing Problems to Building Solutions,” was about the skill nobody teaches engineers: finding the problem before solving it.

Watch the talk

What if we were never taught how to find the right problems?

The talk

Curiosity comes before the solution.

As an engineering student I’ve spent a lot of time building solutions. But my most useful ideas began with something simpler: noticing something that didn’t make sense and asking why.

In a world where AI hands out answers instantly, the ability to ask a better question is becoming the scarce skill. The talk lays out the five-step Builder’s Mindset I use for every invention.
