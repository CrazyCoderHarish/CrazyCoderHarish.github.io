PLUME finds what it can’t see.

A small autonomous robot that searches for a hidden gas leak, works out where it’s coming from, triggers a shutoff and then checks that the leak is really gone. We built it in 36 hours through sensor surprises, failed runs and last-minute redesigns, and made the final round.
