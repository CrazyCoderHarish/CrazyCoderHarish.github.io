Process

From hunch
to publication.

Most ideas die along the way, and that’s the point. Here’s the loop each surviving idea goes through.

01

Find the gap

Start from a real failure: a reading that drifts, a chip that lies, a filter that dies early.

02

Kill it early

Search prior art hard. If it already exists, drop it or find the part that doesn’t.

03

Prove the mechanism

Model and simulate until the physics, not the wording, carries the novelty.

04

Draft & file

Write the specification and claims with mentors, then file through VIT’s IPR cell.
